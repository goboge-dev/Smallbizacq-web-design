How We Work

A Straightforward Process, Built on Trust

We know a business sale is personal. Our process is designed to be direct, respectful of your time, and confidential from the first conversation.

STEP 01

Introductory Call

A no-obligation conversation to learn about your business and understand your goals.

STEP 02

Preliminary Review

We review basic financials under a signed NDA and share initial thoughts on structure and value.

STEP 03

Letter of Intent

We move quickly to a clear, fair offer with terms that reflect your business and your priorities.

STEP 04

Closing & Transition

We work on a timeline that suits you, with a transition plan that protects your team and legacy.
